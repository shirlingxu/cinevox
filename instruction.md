Idea: Film Review & News Podcast App

Goal: Aggregate written movie reviews from platforms like Letterboxd and Reddit, along with the latest movie news, and automatically convert them into a 10–20 minute podcast.

Target Audience: Aspiring film enthusiasts who want a quick, curated audio summary of reviews and news without reading multiple sources.

Why It’s Valuable:
	•	Saves time for users who want to stay updated on movies.
	•	Converts written reviews into engaging, AI-generated narration, making the content more accessible.
	•	Provides a personalized podcast experience based on preferred genres, directors, or trending films.

Gemini / DeepMind Usage:
	•	Text Summarization: Analyze multiple reviews and news articles to extract key opinions and highlights.
	•	Sentiment Analysis: Highlight positive/negative critiques and trending opinions.
	•	Narration Generation: Convert summaries into natural, engaging spoken audio.
	•	Long-Context Memory: Maintain awareness of trending topics and recurring movie franchises to create continuity across podcasts.

Build Stack (Prototype Version):
	•	Frontend: Streamlit or Gradio for user selection of genres/news topics.
	•	Backend: Gemini API for summarization, sentiment, and text-to-speech.
	•	Audio: Lyria or any AI TTS model for generating the podcast.
	•	Optional: Firebase for storing user preferences and podcast history.

Demo Plan:
	•	Select 1–2 movies as examples.
	•	Aggregate 3–5 reviews + latest news.
	•	Generate a 10–20 minute sample podcast with AI narration, showing sentiment highlights and key points.
